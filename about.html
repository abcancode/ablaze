Learn More About
Ablaze

The Ablaze Impact Foundation is a social enterprise, born out of the intention to give back. Set up as an outreach of the Ablaze Bible Study Community, the main objective is to impact the lives of the younger generation. The Foundation with registered office in Lagos, has a membership base spread across Continents.

Brief Overview

Mission

To empower the younger generation, through educational and vocational pathways, providing financial and moral support; and also, character formation, while nurturing them to become responsible, innovative, and impactful leaders in society.

Vision

Impacting the younger generation via social enterprise, by creating opportunities for education, financial support, and character formation that empower them to become self-reliant leaders and builders of sustainable communities.

Core Focus

A future where every young person, regardless of background, has access to quality education, financial support, and moral guidance to unlock their full potential and become responsible leaders in society.

Organizational Structure

The Foundation is comprised of the Trustees, who will have strategic responsibility for delivering the vision.The advisory Board shall be comprised of independently selected individuals, meeting certain criteria approved by the Board of Trustees. Our organization is built on a foundation of collaboration, transparency, and leadership. Together, we ensure our mission and vision are achieved.
