Programs

Scholarship Programs

Ablaze Impact Foundation believes that every young person deserves a fair chance at education, regardless of their financial background. Through our scholarship programs, we provide full or partial funding to support students who demonstrate passion, potential, and resilience but lack the resources to pursue quality education.

Apply Now Partner with Us
Requirements

Scholarship Eligibility

Eligible candidates should be enrolled in or seeking admission to an accredited educational institution, possess a track record of dedication to learning, and embody the values of integrity, resilience, and leadership.

How to Apply

Step 1

Complete the Online Application Form

Begin by filling out the official scholarship application form on our website. Provide accurate personal details, educational background, and a brief statement outlining your financial need and academic goals

Step 2

Submit Supporting Documents

Upload all required documents, including your admission letter (or proof of enrollment), recent academic transcript, and a recommendation letter from a teacher, mentor, or community leader.

Step 3

Application Review & Shortlisting

Our selection committee carefully reviews all applications, focusing on academic merit, financial need, and personal motivation. Shortlisted candidates will be contacted for a brief virtual or in-person interview.

Step 4

Scholarship Award & Onboarding

Successful applicants will receive a full tuition scholarship and a monthly upkeep allowance of ₦100,000. Recipients will also participate in a short onboarding session to understand the program’s expectations and support structure.

Sponsor a future

Adopt a Student

The ‘Adopt a Student’ platform in the Ablaze Impact Foundation, was created to provide an option for interested donors to sponsor a child through educational or vocational training. The individual shall be responsible for the Student for the duration of their program.

Sponsorship cost

Estimated Sponsorship Cost

We cover tuition fees for each student and, in addition, provide a monthly upkeep allowance of ₦100,000 to support their living expenses. This approach ensures that beneficiaries are not only enrolled in school but also have the financial stability needed to focus on their studies. Taking these commitments into account, we estimated the total cost per student at approximately ₦1,000,000. This figure reflects both the tuition fees and the monthly upkeep.

How It Works

Candidates shall be selected into the program, following review of credentials and assessment of the prospective candidates. Nomination of candidates shall be through diverse verifiable sources, which shall include family members, friends, parishioners, members of congregations, etc. What is important is that the prospective students are verified and can provide valid referees. Candidate shall be at pre-admission stage at the least and First year of their program at the most. Funding shall be for the duration of the program the prospective student is admitted into.

FAQ

Question 1

▾

Q: Who is eligible to apply for the Ablaze Impact Foundation Scholarship?

A: Applicants must be young individuals from low-income backgrounds who demonstrate strong academic potential and a genuine passion for education. Candidates must also be enrolled or seeking admission into a recognized tertiary institution in Nigeria.

Question 2

▾

Q: What does the scholarship cover?

A: The scholarship fully covers tuition fees for each selected student and includes a monthly upkeep allowance of ₦100,000 to support living expenses. This ensures that beneficiaries can focus on their studies without financial stress.

Question 3

▾

Q: How much financial support does each beneficiary receive?

A: Each student receives a total support package worth approximately ₦1,000,000 per academic year, which includes tuition and monthly upkeep.

Question 4

▾

Q:How can I apply for the scholarship?

A: Applicants can apply by completing the online scholarship application form available on the Ablaze Impact Foundation website. The process includes submitting personal information, proof of financial need, and academic records. Shortlisted candidates will be contacted for interviews or further assessments.

Question 5

▾

Q: When will successful applicants be notified?

A: After the application deadline, all submissions are reviewed carefully. Successful candidates will be contacted via email or phone within four to six weeks of the application closing date.
