Sponsor a future

Adopt a Student

The ‘Adopt a Student’ platform in the Ablaze Impact Foundation, was created to provide an option for interested donors to sponsor a child through educational or vocational training. The individual shall be responsible for the Student for the duration of their program.

Sponsorship cost

Estimated School/Sponsorship Costs

We cover tuition fees for each student and, in addition, provide a monthly upkeep allowance of ₦100,000 to support their living expenses. This approach ensures that beneficiaries are not only enrolled in school but also have the financial stability needed to focus on their studies. Taking these commitments into account, we estimated the total cost per student at approximately ₦1,000,000. This figure reflects both the tuition fees and the monthly upkeep, giving us a clear picture of the overall financial support allocated to each beneficiary.

How It Works

Candidates shall be selected into the program, following review of credentials and assessment of the prospective candidates. Nomination of candidates shall be through diverse verifiable sources, which shall include family members, friends, parishioners, members of congregations, …etc.; what is important is that the prospective students are verified and can provide valid referees. Candidate shall be at pre-admission stage at the least and First year of their program at the most. Funding shall be for the duration of the program the prospective student is admitted into.
