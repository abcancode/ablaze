Ablaze Impact Foundation

Setting the world on fire inspired by the Holy Spirit

The Ablaze Impact Foundation is a social enterprise born out of a desire to give back, established as an outreach of the Ablaze Bible Study Community. Guided by the Holy Spirit and founded on the Word of God, Ablaze is a Christian community committed to deepening our knowledge of God through Bible study, seminars, and talks.

Brief Overview

Mission

To empower the younger generation, through educational and vocational pathways, providing financial and moral support; and also, character formation, while nurturing them to become responsible, innovative, and impactful leaders in society.

Vision

Impacting the younger generation via social enterprise, by creating opportunities for education, financial support, and character formation that empower them to become self-reliant leaders and builders of sustainable communities.

Core Focus

A future where every young person, regardless of background, has access to quality education, financial support, and moral guidance to unlock their full potential and become responsible leaders in society.

About Us

Who We Are

Guided by the Holy Spirit and founded on the Word of God, Ablaze is a Christian community committed to deepening our knowledge of God through Bible study, seminars, and talks. With its registered office in Lagos and a membership base spread across continents, the Foundation’s main objective is to impact the lives of the younger generation while creating a vibrant community for those seeking to grow in their understanding of God’s Word.
